Ammi · Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who uses Ammi. Ammi is used only by its owner, with his own Google account. It has no other users.

What Ammi accesses. With the owner's permission, given on Google's sign-in screen, Ammi can access his Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Sheets and Google Docs. It asks for read access first; any permission to change or send something is requested separately.

How the data is used. The data is used only to do what the owner asks, such as finding an email, summarising a document or listing calendar events. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to build profiles.

Where the data goes. Ammi runs on the owner's own computer. Google sign-in tokens are stored in the computer's macOS Keychain. When the owner asks an AI assistant (such as Claude or Codex) to work with his Google data, the relevant data is sent to that assistant's provider to produce the answer. It is not shared with anyone else.

Limited Use. Ammi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. The owner can remove Ammi's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact. The support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Ammi.
